### ✦ What we build

Small, focused apps that do one thing well: fast, private by default, and free of clutter. Mostly web apps and installable PWAs, with desktop and Android apps where a native experience makes sense.

### ✦ Principles

- **Privacy first** — only the permissions an app truly needs, and we never sell your data.
- **Craft over features** — polished, accessible design that feels at home on every platform it runs on.
- **Built to last** — modern web and native tooling, careful testing, steady updates.
